01 / About

I'm Carlos, a Platform Engineer at Volkswagen Digital:Hub. Since 2022, I've worked with DevOps practices, cloud infrastructure, and the systems that take code from development to production.

My curiosity started with Windows 3.1, led me to Linux in 2008, and has kept me exploring ever since. I care about understanding how things work, automating repetitive tasks, and keeping solutions as simple as they need to be.

These days, I'm working with AWS, CI/CD, and container platforms, while exploring how AI can fit into engineering workflows.
