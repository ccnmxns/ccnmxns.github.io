## The journey

Carlos Cañellas is a Platform Engineer, currently working at Volkswagen Digital:Hub. He's been doing DevOps and PE pipelining and architectural solutions since 2022.

But his love affair with technology started long before, just back after being a toddler, as he was experimenting with a computer with Windows 3.1, back in the old days of computing.

In 2008, as a curiosity-driven teenager, he started up his machine with a CD with a Linux ISO burnt in it and he fell in love with it and the different Unix systems, as he got a Mac in 2010. Since then, he has explored almost all Linux distributions out in the wild, and learnt that in IT, curiosity is key.

He did a professional degree of system administration and networking management at IES Ingeniero de la Cierva in a dual language mode. Also he started the Computer Science degree at University of Murcia, but later on he stepped aside, leaving it in an hiatus.

He is currently learning more about AI, and how to integrate them into pipelines.
